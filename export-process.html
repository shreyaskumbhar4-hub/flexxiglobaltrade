Export Process & Logistics Flow

A completely transparent 6-stage operational pipeline engineered to protect buyers, ensure zero documentation errors, and expedite customs clearance.

1
Step 01

Buyer Enquiry (RFQ)

The buyer initiates an inquiry specifying commodity grades, required quantity (in Metric Tons or Container loads), preferred packaging configuration, destination seaport, and target delivery timeframe.

Target HS Code Discharge Port Incoterms Required (FOB / CIF / CFR)
2
Step 02

Product Sourcing & Lab Verification

Our procurement team connects directly with certified cultivation clusters and processing mills. Representative lot samples are gathered and dispatched to accredited laboratories for COA verification.

Origin Traceability Pre-Shipment Analysis Lot Allocation
3
Step 03

Commercial Quotation & Proforma Invoice (PI)

We provide a transparent Proforma Invoice stating exact unit pricing, Incoterm conditions, agreed payment parameters (Irrevocable LC at sight or confirmed TT), shipment schedule, and bank coordination.

Proforma Invoice Contract Signing LC / TT Financial Terms
4
Step 04

Export Documentation Suite

Preparation of the complete statutory export document package required for seamless customs clearance at both origin and destination ports.

Commercial Invoice
Packing List (Detailed)
Certificate of Origin
Phytosanitary Certificate
Certificate of Analysis (COA)
Bill of Lading (B/L)
5
Step 05

Shipping & Logistics Coordination

Booking freight containers with premier ocean shipping lines (e.g., Maersk, MSC, CMA CGM). Coordination of container stuffing, weighing (VGM), custom house agent (CHA) filing, and port gate-in.

Nhava Sheva (JNPT) Mundra Port
6
Step 06

Delivery Coordination & Real-Time Tracking

Immediate sharing of vessel tracking numbers, container seal numbers, and courier dispatch of original shipping documents via DHL/FedEx for quick destination clearing.

Vessel Tracking Express Courier
