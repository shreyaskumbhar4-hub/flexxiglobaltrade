Operational Diligence

Direct Procurement Without Middlemen Markup

By operating as a nimble, ethical merchant exporter, Flexxi Global Trade eliminates unnecessary brokerage layers. We procure directly at the farm-gate and processing mill level across Gujarat, Maharashtra, Rajasthan, and Punjab.

  • Farm-to-container traceability for agricultural origins
  • Maximum freshness and unadulterated ingredient purity
  • Highly competitive FOB and CIF international port rates

The Merchant Exporter Advantage

Unlike a single factory limited to its own machine capacity, our merchant exporter model enables us to curate multiple grades and package styles for our global buyers from the top-rated certified mills in India.

Accredited Laboratory COA Gate

We never guess quality. Every single export lot is drawn and submitted to ISO/IEC 17025 accredited analytical laboratories for Certificate of Analysis (COA) generation before container stuffing is approved.

Purity Verification

Strict Multi-Parameter Quality Verification

International food safety regulations demand rigorous documentation. We provide buyers with lot-verified physical, chemical, and microbiological reports.

  • Moisture, ash, and mesh fineness verification
  • Microbiological screening (Salmonella, E. coli, Yeast/Mold)
  • Aflatoxin compliance for European & North American limits
